About

I lead sales and innovation at Envnt, a Riyadh-based digital transformation company and OutSystems Global Best Partner. I joined in 2010 as an account owner and grew with the company through manager, director and now vice president.

My work sits between the boardroom and the build. I sit with C-level sponsors to frame the decision they need to make, then I stay close to the architecture, the proof of concept and the commercial structure until the program is live. Most of my clients are national authorities, regulators, defense and healthcare organisations.

Approach

Requirements first, technology second.

1

Start with the decision

Every engagement starts with the decision the sponsor has to make and the number that proves it was right. The technology choice comes after.

2

Slice by outcome

AI agents and modules are scoped by the outcome they own, not by the department they sit in. That keeps accountability clear.

3

Pay for acceptance

Commercial terms tie payment to accepted use cases, so client and vendor win on the same milestone.

4

Humans keep the gate

Agents recommend; people approve. Decision gates are designed in from day one, not added after an audit.

Specialty

Agentic AI on a low-code foundation

Most organisations do not need a research lab. They need AI that plugs into the workflows they already run, on data that stays where regulation says it must. I design agentic platforms as four layers: a governed data foundation, shared services, specialist agents that each own an outcome, and human decision gates. Built on enterprise low-code, the same foundation carries the second, third and tenth use case without starting over.

  • Data residency and PDPL / NDMO alignment
  • Agents scoped by outcome
  • OutSystems and IBM watsonx delivery
  • Human approval at every decision gate
